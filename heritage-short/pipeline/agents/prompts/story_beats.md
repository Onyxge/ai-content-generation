Step 2 of 5: the beat sheet.

Using the outline, break the story into three to five beats, in order. A beat is a small unit of story in which something changes: a decision, a surprise, a feeling. For each beat give an id (b1, b2…), a one-sentence summary, the feeling it should carry, and one picture that could carry it.

The person who owns this story will read the beat sheet before anything else is made. Make each beat concrete enough that they can say "yes, that happened" or "no, it was like this".
