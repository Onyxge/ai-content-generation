Step 1 of 5: the outline.

Read the brief and the audience and intent below. Write:
- a short working title;
- a logline: one sentence that sums up the story;
- a treatment: four to six sentences of plain prose describing the whole film, beginning to end;
- the cast: every person the brief names who should appear or be spoken about, with an id (lower case, letters, digits and underscores, starting with a letter), their role, a visual description an image model can follow (age, build, hair, clothing, one distinctive detail), and whether they are a real person. Mark someone as not real only if the brief says they are invented. Copy a consent id (C1, C2…) only if the brief gives one for that person;
- the look: one line describing the visual style (medium, palette, light, texture), using qualities rather than any artist's name;
- the music: one line describing the score (instruments, mood, texture), instrumental, gentle enough to sit under a voice;
- the feeling the film should leave, in a few words.
