Step 5 of 5: the Amharic narration.

Translate each English line into natural spoken Amharic, the way a family member would tell it, not a word-for-word rendering. Keep each line about as long, when spoken, as the English. Keep names as the brief gives them, written in Ge'ez script.

Spoken words only, in Ge'ez script; no transliteration, no notes inside the lines. In `notes`, list any name or word whose pronunciation a native speaker should check, and any line where the meaning had to change. A native speaker will check every line before it is voiced.
